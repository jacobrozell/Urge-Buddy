Urge Buddy is a self-help tool for iPhone and iPad. Today, check-in, the urge ride, Progress, and Settings are built to stay readable with the system accessibility settings below.

Supported features

Appearance

Urge Buddy follows the system appearance, or you can choose light or dark in the app.

  • In the app: Settings → Appearance → System, Light, or Dark.
  • Or use iOS Settings → Display & Brightness.

VoiceOver

Tabs, check-in choices, the urge ride, progress rows, and settings actions have accessibility labels.

  • iOS Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver

Larger text

Text uses system styles that scale with your text size. At the largest accessibility sizes, wide screens stay one column so lines do not sit side by side.

  • iOS Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text

Bold Text, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Motion

  • Bold Text and Increase Contrast follow the system settings.
  • Reduce Motion turns the breathing circle into a text cue. The moving background on the urge ride stays off.

Contrast and non-color cues

Colors are checked for contrast. A slip uses an amber mark and words, never red alone. Habit progress uses a shape as well as color.

Known limitations

  • At the largest text sizes, long screens need scrolling on a phone.
  • Urge Buddy has no video. Captions and audio descriptions do not apply.
