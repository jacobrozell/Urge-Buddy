Effective date: November 2, 2026

Urge Buddy ("the app") is a self-help tool for quitting, cutting back, and building habits, published by Jacob Rozell. This policy describes what data the app handles and your choices. The app does not treat, cure, or diagnose anything.

Data we store on your device

The app stores vice and habit names, daily entries, amounts, moods, notes, reasons, plans, and urge records on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). This data stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

Notifications

Daily check-in and milestone reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through a server. Discreet notifications can hide habit and vice names on the lock screen.

App lock

You can lock Urge Buddy with Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or your device passcode. iOS handles that check. Urge Buddy never receives biometric data.

Export and import

You may export your data as a JSON file and share it through the system share sheet, or import a JSON file you previously exported. Export and import start only when you tap them. The app does not upload exports.

Links you open

Crisis numbers, findahelpline.com, and the urge-surfing reading links open outside the app when you tap them. Urge Buddy does not send your log with those links.

Data we do not collect

  • No account
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No sale of personal information
  • No analytics SDKs

Diagnostics

Release builds use Apple's unified logging (OSLog) on the device for error diagnostics. Habit and vice content is not sent off the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Export a backup — Settings → Export backup saves a JSON file you control.
  • Delete all data — Settings → Delete all data removes vices, habits, and history on that device.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
