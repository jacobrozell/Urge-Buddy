### Description

Here when you need a Buddy. When an urge hits, you surf it, and the app stays with you.

An urge is a wave. It gets stronger, crests, then fades on its own. Breathe with the circle, watch it, and read why this matters to you. You can let it pass without acting on it. Add 5 minutes if it is still up.

Check in once a day. It takes about 10 seconds. Quit what's hurting you and build what helps. If you slip, your total free days and the money you've saved stay. A slip restarts the current run. It does not erase the progress you already made.

You can quit, cut back against a daily limit, or only build habits.

Urge Buddy is a self-help tool. It does not treat, cure, or diagnose anything. If you are in crisis, the app links to 988, SAMHSA, and findahelpline.com.

- Quit one or more vices, cut back, or only build habits
- Live count of days free, and a calendar you can correct
- A guided way to surf an urge: timer, breath, your reasons, your plan
- Optional daily reminder. Nothing else
- Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode
- Export a backup you control. Nothing leaves your device unless you share the file
