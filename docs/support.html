Common questions

How do I check in?
Open Today and tap the daily check-in. It asks about each thing you are quitting or cutting back. It takes about 10 seconds.

What do I do when an urge hits?
Tap I'm having an urge on Today. Breathe with the circle, read your reasons, and stay until the wave falls. You can add 5 minutes if it is still up.

How do I delete all my data?
Open Settings → Delete all data and confirm. This permanently removes vices, habits, and history on that device.

How do I back up or restore my data?
Open Settings → Export backup to save a JSON file you control. Use Import backup to restore it.

Does the app require an account?
No. Urge Buddy works offline with data stored on your device.

Does the app show ads?
No. Urge Buddy is free and ad-free.

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone or iPad. See the Privacy Policy.
